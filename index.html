HADI FADHEL - Adaptive Roadmap

"[Slide 19 - Adaptive Roadmap & Progress System]

Let's start with the Adaptive Roadmap.

We built 3 independent career paths: Frontend Development, Backend Development, and Cybersecurity. Each path is broken into numbered levels with specific topics. And here's the critical part: levels lock sequentially. You can't skip ahead. Why? Because in real learning, you can't master React before you understand JavaScript fundamentals. The platform enforces that structure.

Completed levels display a checkmark. Your active level is highlighted with direct shortcuts to quiz, flashcard, and cheatsheet. And when you complete a level, milestone badges unlock to keep you motivated.

Now, the Progress Dashboard ties this all together. Three stat cards show your overall completion percentage, topics completed, and average quiz score. Individual progress bars per specialization with color coding—so if you're 60% through Frontend but only 20% through Backend, you see that instantly. A recent activity feed shows your last actions across all paths. And a badge shelf displays what you've earned versus what's still locked.

All of this is powered by simulated mock data for this prototype—but it's designed to be fully extensible to live data in future iterations."

FEATURES · HADI FADHEL

Feature: Interactive Quiz System

How the Quiz Works

Each quiz is tied to the current roadmap level. Questions are multiple-choice. A progress bar at the top tells you how far through the quiz you are. Answers give immediate feedback before moving to the next question.

Unanswered State

Question displayed with 4 answer options. All options neutral styling. Progress indicator shows position (e.g. Question 2 of 8). Score tracker visible top-right.

Correct Answer

Selected option turns green immediately. Short explanation appears below explaining WHY the answer is correct. 'Next Question →' button activates.

Wrong Answer

Selected option turns red. The correct answer is highlighted in green simultaneously. Learner still sees the right answer — learning from mistakes is the goal.

End of Quiz: Score summary screen (X/Y correct) before unlocking next level.

HADI FADHEL - Quiz System

"[Slide 20 - Interactive Quiz System]

Now let's talk about the Quiz System, because this is where learning becomes active.

Each quiz is tied to your current roadmap level. Questions are multiple-choice. A progress bar at the top tells you how far through the quiz you are. And answers give immediate feedback before moving to the next question.

Three states: Unanswered—question displayed, all options neutral. Correct Answer—selected option turns green, short explanation appears below explaining why the answer is correct. HADI FADHEL - Flashcards & Cheatsheet

"[Slide 21 - Flashcards & Cheatsheet]

For revision, we built two tools: Flashcards and Cheatsheets.

Flashcards are flip-card interactions—term on front, definition on back. They're scoped to your active roadmap topic, so you're always reviewing what's relevant. Previous/Next navigation, position counter (Card 2 of 8), designed for rapid revision.

Cheatsheets are quick-reference pages you can open without leaving your roadmap position.
